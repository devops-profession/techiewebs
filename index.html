About TechieWebs

Bangalore's Open-Source
Development Studio

Born in 2025 from a shared belief that great software should be transparent, affordable, and fully owned by you. We're a lean team of developers, designers, and architects who've traded corporate comfort for startup speed — building products for startups, SMEs, and growth-stage businesses across India and beyond.

  • Open-source by default. React, Node.js, Django, PostgreSQL — no proprietary lock-in, ever.
  • Agile delivery. Two-week sprints, weekly demos, and full code ownership transferred at launch.
  • Bangalore-based, globally minded. IST timezone, English fluency, clients across Asia, Europe, and the US.
  • Security & accessibility first. OWASP practices and WCAG 2.1 compliance built into every project.
  • Post-launch partnership. We don't disappear after go-live. Retainer and AMC plans available.
